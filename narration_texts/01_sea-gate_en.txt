Welcome to Kotor. Before you cross this threshold, look up at the heavy stone arch of the Sea Gate. The entrance you see today dates from 1555, when the city was governed by Venice. Imagine approaching these walls centuries ago. The water lay much closer to the gate, and arriving in Kotor often meant arriving by boat. For a maritime town, the bay was both a highway to the wider world and a source of danger. Behind the walls, merchants, craftspeople and sailors built a life in streets you can still walk. Take a moment to notice the contrast: mountains behind you, stone ahead, and a whole city waiting on the other side. When you're ready, step through the gate.
