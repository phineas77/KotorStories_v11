## 01. Sea Gate: Welcome to Kotor / Morska vrata: dobro došli u Kotor

**Zona:** Morska vrata · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 50 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Sea Gate is Kotor's principal western entrance. Its present form dates to 1555, during the administration of the Venetian governor Bernardo Renier. The Republic of Venice governed Kotor from 1420 until 1797, a period that strongly influenced its public buildings and fortifications. The entrance is framed by large blocks of dressed stone and a monumental arch. Its position also preserves the relationship between the walled town and the bay: before the modern waterfront was developed, vessels could approach much closer to the city. Kotor's harbour connected it to trading ports throughout the Adriatic. Goods arrived by sea, while the city supplied ships with craftsmen, sailors and merchants. The Sea Gate therefore served two purposes: a controlled point of entry into a defended settlement and a passage between the harbour and the commercial centre.

**ME — naracija**

Morska vrata su glavni zapadni ulaz u Kotor. Njihov današnji oblik potiče iz 1555. godine, iz vremena mletačkog providura Bernarda Renijera. Mletačka republika upravljala je Kotorom od 1420. do 1797. godine, a taj period snažno je uticao na izgled javnih zgrada i utvrđenja. Ulaz je uokviren krupnim, obrađenim kamenim blokovima i monumentalnim lukom. Njegov položaj svjedoči o vezi grada i zaliva: prije izgradnje današnje obale brodovi su mogli pristajati znatno bliže bedemima. Kotorska luka povezivala je grad sa trgovačkim središtima širom Jadrana. Morem je stizala roba, dok su gradske radionice i trgovci opsluživali pomorce. Morska vrata su zato bila i kontrolisani ulaz u utvrđeni grad i veza između luke i trgovačkog središta.

## 02. Sea Gate: Signs of Changing Times / Morska vrata: tragovi različitih vremena

**Zona:** Morska vrata · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Sea Gate belongs to a longer sequence of political changes. Venice governed the city from 1420 to 1797; the first period of Austrian administration followed from 1797 to 1805. Russian administration is documented for 1806 and 1807, and French administration for the years beginning in 1807. These dates are recorded in the holdings of the Historical Archive of Kotor. The gate itself is older than those nineteenth-century changes: its monumental Renaissance-era stonework dates to the sixteenth century. Architecture and inscriptions in the Old Town should therefore be read in their historical setting rather than attributed to one ruler or one period. A medieval street plan, Venetian civic buildings and later interventions coexist within the same walls. The entrance is an appropriate introduction to a town whose institutions changed repeatedly while its maritime location remained constant.

**ME — naracija**

Morska vrata su svjedok duge smjene vlasti u Kotoru. Mletačka uprava trajala je od 1420. do 1797, a prva austrijska od 1797. do 1805. godine. U fondovima Istorijskog arhiva Kotora zabilježena je ruska uprava tokom 1806. i 1807, kao i francuska uprava od 1807. godine. Sama vrata starija su od tih promjena: monumentalna kamena obrada potiče iz XVI vijeka. Zato gradske natpise, simbole i građevine treba vezivati za tačno određene epohe, a ne za jednu neprekinutu vlast. Srednjovjekovna mreža ulica, mletačka javna arhitektura i kasnije intervencije danas postoje u istom prostoru. Ovaj ulaz pokazuje kako su se političke institucije mijenjale, dok je Kotor ostajao važna luka na kraju Bokokotorskog zaliva.

## 03. The Walls and the Sea / Bedemi i more

**Zona:** Morska vrata · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 48 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's fortifications were built and altered over many centuries, from approximately the ninth to the eighteenth century. According to the Municipality of Kotor, their total length is about 4.5 kilometres; some sections reach a height of twenty metres. The walls did not protect only the streets at sea level. They also climb the steep slopes towards the fortress of Saint John, approximately 260 metres above sea level. This defensive system combined the harbour, the fortified town and the mountain into a single position. The need for such protection reflected Kotor's value as a commercial port and the military pressures affecting the eastern Adriatic. The wider Natural and Culturo-Historical Region of Kotor was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 1979. Its significance rests on both the historic settlements and the landscape of the bay.

**ME — naracija**

Kotorski bedemi nastajali su i mijenjali se tokom više vjekova, približno od IX do XVIII vijeka. Prema podacima Opštine Kotor, ukupno su dugi oko 4,5 kilometara, a pojedini djelovi dostižu visinu od dvadeset metara. Nijesu štitili samo ulice uz more. Bedemi se penju i uz strmu padinu prema tvrđavi Sveti Ivan, na približno 260 metara nadmorske visine. Luka, utvrđeni grad i planina tako čine jedinstven odbrambeni sistem. Snažna zaštita bila je potrebna zbog trgovačke važnosti Kotora i čestih vojnih sukoba na istočnoj obali Jadrana. Prirodno i kulturno-istorijsko područje Kotora upisano je na UNESCO-vu Listu svjetske baštine 1979. godine. Njegova vrijednost obuhvata i istorijska naselja i izuzetni pejzaž zaliva.

## 04. Square of Arms: The Heart of the Town / Trg od oružja: srce grada

**Zona:** Trg od oružja · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Square of Arms is the largest square within Kotor's medieval street network. Its name recalls the military role of the space, especially during the centuries of Venetian administration, from 1420 to 1797. Public buildings associated with the governor and the city guard faced the square, making it a centre of administration as well as public activity. Two of the square's important landmarks are the Clock Tower, constructed in 1602, and the nearby stone structure known as the pillory. The main entrance through the Sea Gate opens directly towards this area, linking the harbour to the city's institutions. Kotor was not organized around a single ceremonial avenue. Instead, narrow streets led into a series of squares, each serving different functions. The Square of Arms was the principal setting for civic authority and public announcements.

**ME — naracija**

Trg od oružja najveći je trg unutar srednjovjekovne mreže kotorskih ulica. Njegovo ime podsjeća na vojnu ulogu ovog prostora, posebno tokom mletačke uprave od 1420. do 1797. godine. Prema trgu su bile okrenute zgrade povezane sa providurom i gradskom stražom, pa je predstavljao upravno i javno središte. Među njegovim važnim znamenitostima su Sat kula, podignuta 1602. godine, i kamena građevina poznata kao stub srama. Glavni ulaz kroz Morska vrata vodi neposredno prema ovom prostoru i povezuje luku sa gradskim ustanovama. Kotor nije izgrađen oko jedne široke ulice, već oko niza trgova povezanih uskim prolazima. Trg od oružja bio je najvažnije mjesto na kojem su se predstavljali gradska vlast i javne odluke.

## 05. The Clock Tower / Sat kula

**Zona:** Trg od oružja · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 63 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's Clock Tower was erected in 1602 during the term of the Venetian governor Antonio Grimaldi. His initials and the year of construction are associated with the stone inscription above its entrance. The tower was built from large, roughly rusticated stone blocks, with an open upper level for the bell and clock mechanism. A record from 1788 already mentions a clock mechanism with weights and a dial facing the Sea Gate. This is important because an older theory attributed the first installation of the clock to the French period of 1807–1814. The tower was damaged and tilted during earthquakes, including the one in 1979; subsequent conservation works stabilized its foundations. Restoration of the tower, bell and nearby pillory was also carried out in 2020. Its present appearance is therefore the result of construction, repair and continued use over more than four centuries.

**ME — naracija**

Kotorska Sat kula podignuta je 1602. godine, za vrijeme mletačkog providura Antonija Grimaldija. Njegovi inicijali i godina gradnje povezani su sa kamenim natpisom iznad ulaza. Kula je zidana masivnim kamenim kvaderima obrađenim u tehnici bunjato, a na vrhu ima otvoreni prostor za zvono i satni mehanizam. Zapis iz 1788. godine već pominje mehanizam sa tegovima i brojčanikom okrenutim prema Morskim vratima. To je važno jer se ranije smatralo da je prvi sat postavljen tek za francuske uprave, od 1807. do 1814. Zemljotresi, naročito onaj iz 1979, oštetili su kulu i izazvali njeno naginjanje. Kasniji konzervatorski radovi učvrstili su temelje, a 2020. godine obnavljani su kula, zvono i susjedni stub srama.

## 06. The Pillory and the Public Square / Stub srama i život na trgu

**Zona:** Trg od oružja · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 64 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The stone monument in front of the Clock Tower is commonly called the pillory, or Stub srama. Local tradition associates this place with the public display of punishment and civic authority. The present stone structure has been restored, so it should not be treated as an untouched medieval object. A historical study of the tower notes that the pillory is known partly through local tradition, rather than a complete record of particular sentences carried out here. The contrast between the two monuments is revealing: the tower, built in 1602, regulated public time, while the space nearby represented the authority to enforce rules. Both belonged to the civic life of a fortified trading town. The Municipality of Kotor carried out conservation work on the pillory and the Clock Tower in 2020. The structure therefore also illustrates the modern effort to preserve Kotor's public monuments.

**ME — naracija**

Kameni spomenik ispred Sat kule poznat je kao stub srama. Prema lokalnom predanju, to mjesto bilo je povezano sa javnim pokazivanjem kazni i autoriteta gradske vlasti. Današnja kamena građevina je obnavljana, pa se ne može predstavljati kao netaknut srednjovjekovni predmet. U istorijskom prikazu Sat kule navodi se da se funkcija stuba dijelom oslanja na predanje, a ne na sačuvan potpuni spisak kazni izvršenih na tom mjestu. Odnos dva spomenika ipak je zanimljiv: kula, podignuta 1602, uređivala je javno vrijeme, dok je prostor ispred nje simbolizovao primjenu gradskih pravila. Oba su pripadala građanskom životu utvrđene trgovačke zajednice. Opština Kotor sprovela je konzervatorske radove na stubu srama i Sat kuli 2020. godine.

## 07. Venetian Kotor and its Palaces / Mletački Kotor i njegove palate

**Zona:** Trg od oružja · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Bizanti Palace demonstrates how a medieval noble house could be transformed over generations. The building originated in the fourteenth century and has a façade facing the Square of Arms. After the earthquake of 1667, the palace underwent substantial alterations. A coat of arms and inscription associated with Nikola Bizanti record work carried out in 1674. Its wings are joined by an internal courtyard and staircase, while portals, windows and well details reveal Renaissance and Baroque influences. The palace also reflects the social structure of Kotor under Venetian rule between 1420 and 1797. Noble families retained prominent positions in the city's economic and civic life, even as the republic controlled the wider political framework. The building should be read as a sequence of changes, not as a perfectly preserved example of a single architectural period.

**ME — naracija**

Palata Bizanti pokazuje kako se jedna srednjovjekovna plemićka kuća mijenjala kroz generacije. Nastala je u XIV vijeku, a jednom fasadom izlazi na Trg od oružja. Poslije zemljotresa 1667. godine palata je znatno preuređena. Grb i natpis povezani sa Nikolom Bizantijem bilježe radove iz 1674. godine. Njena krila povezana su unutrašnjim dvorištem i stepeništem, a portali, prozori i detalji bunara nose renesansne i barokne osobine. Palata svjedoči i o položaju plemićkih porodica za vrijeme mletačke uprave od 1420. do 1797. godine. One su zadržavale važnu ulogu u gradskom privrednom i javnom životu, iako je širi politički okvir određivala Republika. Zgrada zato nije spomenik samo jednoj epohi, već nizu obnova i prilagođavanja.

## 08. Saint Tryphon: Patron of Kotor / Sveti Tripun: zaštitnik Kotora

**Zona:** Katedrala Sv. Tripuna · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 63 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Saint Tryphon has been honoured as Kotor's patron for more than twelve centuries. According to the city's longstanding tradition, his relics arrived in Kotor in 809. Sources about the original shrine identify the local nobleman Andrija Saracenis with the construction of an early sanctuary dedicated to the saint. The celebration of Saint Tryphon takes place on 3 February, and the events surrounding it are closely connected with the ceremonial traditions of the Boka Navy. The arrival of the relics is also part of the foundation story of that maritime brotherhood, although the date 809 belongs to tradition rather than a surviving founding charter. UNESCO recognized the cultural heritage of the Boka Navy in 2021, and the Saint Tryphon festivities maintained by Boka Croats in Croatia were separately inscribed in 2022. The patron saint therefore links religious devotion, civic memory and maritime identity.

**ME — naracija**

Sveti Tripun poštuje se kao zaštitnik Kotora duže od dvanaest vjekova. Prema dugoj gradskoj tradiciji, njegove mošti stigle su u Kotor 809. godine. Izvori o prvobitnom svetilištu povezuju kotorskog plemića Andriju Saracenisa sa podizanjem rane crkve posvećene svecu. Tripundan se obilježava 3. februara, a svečanosti su tijesno povezane sa ceremonijama Bokeljske mornarice. Dolazak moštiju dio je i predanja o nastanku te pomorske bratovštine, iako je 809. tradicionalna godina, a ne datum iz sačuvane osnivačke povelje. UNESCO je 2021. upisao kulturnu baštinu Bokeljske mornarice na reprezentativnu listu nematerijalne baštine. Godine 2022. posebno su upisane tripundanske svečanosti koje čuvaju bokeljski Hrvati u Hrvatskoj. Sveti Tripun povezuje vjeru, gradsko pamćenje i pomorski identitet.

## 09. A Cathedral Built Across Centuries / Katedrala koja je rasla kroz vjekove

**Zona:** Katedrala Sv. Tripuna · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 62 s / 52 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Cathedral of Saint Tryphon was built on the site of an earlier ninth-century sanctuary. Construction of the present monumental Romanesque church began in 1124, and it was consecrated in 1166. Its plan is that of a three-aisled basilica with a wider central nave. The original western façade and bell towers were substantially altered after the earthquake of 1667, when Baroque forms were introduced during rebuilding. This accounts for the combination of Romanesque structure and later details visible today. The cathedral was not merely a place of worship. Its treasury, stone carving and historic artworks reflect the wealth and craftsmanship of medieval and early modern Kotor. Research and conservation following the earthquake of 1979 also helped clarify the building's earlier phases. More than eight centuries after its consecration, it remains the most prominent historic church in the Old Town.

**ME — naracija**

Katedrala Svetog Tripuna sagrađena je na mjestu starijeg svetilišta iz IX vijeka. Gradnja današnje monumentalne romaničke crkve počela je 1124, a osvećena je 1166. godine. U osnovi je trobrodna bazilika sa širim centralnim brodom. Prvobitna zapadna fasada i zvonici znatno su izmijenjeni poslije zemljotresa 1667. godine, kada su tokom obnove uvedeni barokni oblici. Zato se na današnjoj građevini prepliću romanička konstrukcija i kasniji arhitektonski detalji. Katedrala nije bila samo bogomolja. Njena riznica, kamenorezački ukrasi i umjetnička djela svjedoče o bogatstvu i zanatskom umijeću srednjovjekovnog i novovjekovnog Kotora. Istraživanja i konzervacija nakon zemljotresa 1979. pomogli su da se bolje razumiju starije faze gradnje. Više od osam vjekova poslije osvećenja, katedrala je i dalje najpoznatiji istorijski hram Starog grada.

## 10. Earthquakes That Changed the Skyline / Zemljotresi koji su mijenjali grad

**Zona:** Katedrala Sv. Tripuna · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 55 s / 50 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Earthquakes repeatedly reshaped Kotor's architecture. A major earthquake in 1667 caused widespread damage along the eastern Adriatic and affected several of Kotor's most important buildings. The cathedral's original Romanesque towers and western façade were altered in subsequent reconstruction. The Bizanti Palace was substantially changed after the same disaster, with building work recorded in 1674. Another devastating earthquake struck the Montenegrin coast on 15 April 1979. It damaged churches, palaces and fortifications, including the Maritime Museum's home in Grgurina Palace. Reconstruction required not only masonry repairs but also research into older building phases and the protection of art collections. The town's present appearance is therefore not a frozen medieval image. It includes deliberate restorations and replacements made after documented disasters in 1667 and 1979.

**ME — naracija**

Zemljotresi su više puta mijenjali arhitekturu Kotora. Veliki potres iz 1667. godine izazvao je teška oštećenja na istočnoj obali Jadrana i pogodio neke od najvažnijih kotorskih građevina. Prvobitni romanički zvonici i zapadna fasada katedrale izmijenjeni su tokom kasnije obnove. I Palata Bizanti znatno je promijenjena poslije iste katastrofe, a radovi su zabilježeni 1674. godine. Novi razorni zemljotres pogodio je crnogorsko primorje 15. aprila 1979. Oštetio je crkve, palate i bedeme, uključujući i Palatu Grgurina u kojoj se nalazi Pomorski muzej. Obnova nije značila samo popravku zidova: istraživane su starije faze gradnje i zaštićivane umjetničke zbirke. Današnji izgled Kotora zato uključuje i pažljive restauracije i zamjene izvedene nakon katastrofa iz 1667. i 1979. godine.

## 11. Faith, Art and the Goldsmiths / Vjera, umjetnost i kotorski zlatari

**Zona:** Katedrala Sv. Tripuna · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 57 s / 54 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's artistic tradition is especially visible in the treasury of Saint Tryphon's Cathedral. It preserves works from different centuries, including liturgical objects made by local goldsmiths between the fourteenth and eighteenth centuries. The fourteenth-century Romanesque-Gothic ciborium above the main altar is one of the cathedral's important medieval features. Its collection also includes paintings associated with the fifteenth-century artist Lovro Marinov Dobričević and the seventeenth-century painter Tripo Kokolja. Wall-painting fragments reflect artistic connections beyond Kotor, including masters described in historical sources as Greek painters. These works reveal that the city was not simply a port where goods passed through. It was a centre in which specialised crafts and visual traditions developed. The artistic importance of the region's medieval masonry and painting schools is specifically recognized in UNESCO's description of Kotor.

**ME — naracija**

Umjetnička tradicija Kotora posebno je vidljiva u riznici Katedrale Svetog Tripuna. U njoj se čuvaju radovi iz različitih vjekova, uključujući liturgijske predmete koje su domaći zlatari izrađivali od XIV do XVIII vijeka. Romaničko-gotički ciborijum iz XIV vijeka, iznad glavnog oltara, jedno je od važnih srednjovjekovnih ostvarenja. ## 12. Maritime Museum: A City of Sailors / Pomorski muzej: grad pomoraca

**Zona:** Pomorski muzej · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 63 s / 53 s (135 riječi/min)

The Maritime Museum of Montenegro grew from a collection assembled by the Boka Navy brotherhood around 1880. It was opened to the public in 1900 and moved into rooms of the Grgurina Palace in 1938. The institution was formally reorganized after the Second World War, and the palace was adapted for museum use between 1949 and 1952. ## 13. Grgurina Palace: Life Behind the Façade / Palata Grgurina: život iza fasade

## 14. From the Bay to the Wider World / Iz Boke prema velikom svijetu

**Zona:** Pomorski muzej · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 62 s / 52 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

A striking example of Boka's global connections is the captain Ivo Visin, born in Prčanj in 1806. Commanding the brig Splendido, he departed Antwerp on 11 February 1852 and completed a voyage around the world in 1859. The journey carried his ship past Cape Horn, across the Pacific, through Asian waters and around the Cape of Good Hope. The voyage became an important achievement in the history of the Austrian merchant marine, under whose flag the ship sailed. In 1860, Visin received the honorary Merito navali flag for his accomplishment. This was not an isolated adventure but an exceptional example of a wider maritime culture. Kotor and nearby coastal settlements produced captains, navigators and merchants who worked far beyond the Adriatic. Money, knowledge, books and styles brought back through maritime trade shaped houses, churches and everyday life in the bay.

**ME — naracija**

Primjer svjetskih veza Boke je kapetan Ivo Visin, rođen u Prčanju 1806. godine. Zapovijedajući brikom Splendido, isplovio je iz Antverpena 11. februara 1852. i završio putovanje oko svijeta 1859. godine. Brod je prošao pored Rta Horn, preko Tihog okeana, azijskim morima i oko Rta dobre nade. Putovanje je postalo važan podvig trgovačke mornarice pod austrijskom zastavom, pod kojom je Splendido plovio. Visin je 1860. dobio počasnu zastavu Merito navali. To nije bila izdvojena pustolovina, već izuzetan primjer mnogo šire pomorske kulture. Kotor i obližnja primorska mjesta davali su kapetane, navigatore i trgovce koji su radili daleko izvan Jadrana. Novac, znanje, knjige i novi stilovi pristigli pomorskom trgovinom uticali su na kuće, crkve i svakodnevni život Boke.

## 15. The Boka Navy: A Living Tradition / Bokeljska mornarica: živa tradicija

**Zona:** Pomorski muzej · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 46 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Boka Navy is one of Kotor's best-known living maritime traditions. Its traditional foundation date is 809, linked to the arrival of Saint Tryphon's relics. The earliest surviving written references are much later: a document from 1353 mentions an association of Kotor's seafarers, and a statute from 1463 records the brotherhood of Saint Nicholas of the Sailors. Over time the organization had religious, economic, charitable and military roles. Its modern ceremonial life preserves uniforms, historic weapons and the traditional chain dance known as the kolo. In 2021, UNESCO inscribed the cultural heritage of the Boka Navy on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. The distinction between traditional origin and documentary evidence is significant. It allows Kotor's residents to celebrate a twelve-century-old identity while remaining precise about the surviving historical record.

**ME — naracija**

Bokeljska mornarica jedna je od najpoznatijih živih pomorskih tradicija Kotora. Njena tradicionalna godina osnivanja je 809, povezana sa dolaskom moštiju Svetog Tripuna. Najstarija sačuvana pisana svjedočanstva znatno su mlađa: dokument iz 1353. pominje udruženje kotorskih pomoraca, a statut iz 1463. bratovštinu Svetog Nikole mornara. Organizacija je kroz istoriju imala vjerske, privredne, dobrotvorne i vojne funkcije. Danas njene svečanosti čuvaju tradicionalne uniforme, istorijsko oružje i kolo Bokeljske mornarice. UNESCO je 2021. godine upisao kulturnu baštinu Bokeljske mornarice na Reprezentativnu listu nematerijalne kulturne baštine čovječanstva. Važno je razlikovati tradicionalni početak od datuma sačuvanih dokumenata. Tako se poštuje duboko ukorijenjena kotorska tradicija, uz preciznost prema istorijskim svjedočanstvima.

## 16. The Church of Saint Luke / Crkva Svetog Luke

**Zona:** Trg Sv. Luke · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 56 s / 48 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Church of Saint Luke was built in 1195, as recorded by an inscription associated with its founders, Mauro Caccafrangi and his wife Bona. It is one of the oldest surviving churches within Kotor's walls. The building combines Romanesque forms with elements of Byzantine church architecture, including a compact interior beneath a dome. Fragments of medieval wall painting survive, providing evidence of its earlier decoration. The seventeenth-century iconostasis is associated with Dimitrije Daskal, an important figure in the Boka icon-painting tradition. The building survived the major earthquakes that transformed many neighbouring churches, making it valuable for the study of Kotor's medieval architecture. It also occupies a central place in the religious history of the town, as its use changed between Catholic and Orthodox communities during later centuries.

**ME — naracija**

Crkva Svetog Luke sagrađena je 1195. godine, o čemu svjedoči natpis povezan sa njenim ktitorima, Maurom Kacafrangijem i njegovom suprugom Bonom. Jedna je od najstarijih sačuvanih crkava unutar kotorskih zidina. Građevina spaja romaničke oblike i elemente vizantijske crkvene arhitekture, uključujući zbijen prostor pod kupolom. Sačuvani su djelovi srednjovjekovnog zidnog slikarstva koji svjedoče o nekadašnjem ukrasu. Ikonostas iz XVII vijeka povezuje se sa Dimitrijem Daskalom, važnom ličnošću bokeljske ikonopisne tradicije. Crkva je preživjela velike zemljotrese koji su izmijenili brojne okolne hramove, pa je značajna za proučavanje srednjovjekovne arhitekture Kotora. Posebno mjesto ima i u vjerskoj istoriji grada, jer se njena upotreba tokom kasnijih vjekova mijenjala između katoličke i pravoslavne zajednice.

## 17. Two Traditions Under One Roof / Dvije tradicije pod jednim krovom

**Zona:** Trg Sv. Luke · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 59 s / 48 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Church of Saint Luke reflects the changing relationship between Kotor's Catholic and Orthodox communities. It was originally used for Catholic worship. During the seventeenth century, a period of regional warfare and population movement, it was made available to Orthodox believers. A Catholic altar was retained, so both liturgical traditions were represented within the same small building for part of its history. Accounts differ in the details of how long this arrangement lasted, but they agree on the importance of the two altars. The church is now Orthodox. The story is often presented as a symbol of coexistence, but it is also a record of changing political conditions, migration and religious institutions. Its foundation date, 1195, and its later seventeenth-century history belong to different phases, both essential to understanding this remarkable building.

**ME — naracija**

Crkva Svetog Luke odražava promjenljive odnose katoličke i pravoslavne zajednice u Kotoru. U početku je služila katoličkom bogosluženju. Tokom XVII vijeka, u doba regionalnih ratova i pomjeranja stanovništva, ustupljena je na korišćenje pravoslavnim vjernicima. Katolički oltar je zadržan, pa su dvije liturgijske tradicije u jednom razdoblju bile prisutne u istoj maloj crkvi. Izvori se razlikuju u pojedinostima o trajanju takvog uređenja, ali se slažu o postojanju dva oltara. Danas je crkva pravoslavna. Ova istorija često se navodi kao primjer suživota, ali je i svjedočanstvo političkih okolnosti, seoba i razvoja vjerskih ustanova. Osnivanje iz 1195. i događaji iz XVII vijeka pripadaju različitim fazama, podjednako važnim za razumijevanje građevine.

## 18. Saint Luke Square: Kotor Carnival Traditions / Trg Svetog Luke: kotorski karnevalski običaji

**Zona:** Trg Sv. Luke · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 52 s / 47 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's squares have long been places of public gatherings, fairs and carnival celebrations. The earliest known archival reference to the carnival is dated 11 February 1508. It appears in a lease contract that refers to a payment due at carnival time, showing that the custom was already part of the annual calendar in the early sixteenth century. The document does not establish when the carnival first began. Over the centuries, masked processions, humorous performances and public tombolas became familiar parts of local festivities. A carnival procession also provided an opportunity to comment on current events through costumes and satire. These customs belong to Kotor's living heritage, maintained by local communities rather than preserved only in museums.

**ME — naracija**

Kotorske pjace odavno su mjesta javnih okupljanja, sajmova i karnevalskih svečanosti. Najstariji do sada poznati arhivski pomen karnevala datiran je 11. februara 1508. godine. Dokument ne otkriva kada je karneval prvi put održan. Tokom vjekova, maškarane povorke, humoristični nastupi i javne tombole postali su prepoznatljivi djelovi lokalnih fešti. Karneval je bio i prilika da se kroz kostime i satiru komentarišu savremeni događaji. Ovi običaji pripadaju živom nasljeđu Kotora, koje održava lokalna zajednica, a ne samo muzejske zbirke.

## 19. Saint Nicholas: A Different Century / Sveti Nikola: drugačije stoljeće

**EN — narration**

The Church of Saint Nicholas belongs to a much later period than its medieval neighbour, Saint Luke's. Construction of the present Orthodox church began in 1902 and was completed in 1909. The architect was Ćiril Iveković, known for his work on public and religious buildings along the Adriatic. The church's large façade and two bell towers give it a distinctive presence in the northern part of the Old Town. Its iconostasis was completed in 1908 by Czech craftsmen. ## 20. The Northern Gate / Sjeverna vrata

**Zona:** Sjeverna vrata i Škurda · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 52 s / 46 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's northern entrance, also called the River Gate, opens towards the Škurda River. Together with the Sea Gate to the west and the Gurdić entrance to the south, it forms one of the three principal approaches to the walled town. The River Gate was reconstructed in 1540 under Venetian rule, commemorating Kotor's defence against the fleet of Hayreddin Barbarossa in 1539. The entrance once had a drawbridge that connected it with the stone bridge over the Škurda, allowing passage to be restricted in times of danger. Stone coats of arms and inscriptions near the gate preserve the dates 1539 and 1540. The location illustrates how Kotor's fortifications combined masonry, waterways and controlled access points to defend the town.

**ME — naracija**

Sjeverni ulaz u Kotor, poznat i kao Riječna vrata, otvara se prema rijeci Škurdi. Uz Morska vrata na zapadu i ulaz kod Gurdića na jugu, predstavlja jedan od tri glavna prilaza gradu. Riječna vrata obnovljena su 1540. godine, tokom mletačke uprave, u spomen na odbranu grada od flote Hajredina Barbarose 1539. godine. Na ulazu se nekada nalazio pokretni most koji je povezivao vrata sa kamenim mostom preko Škurde i omogućavao zatvaranje prilaza u vrijeme opasnosti. Kameni grbovi i natpisi uz vrata čuvaju datume 1539. i 1540. godine. Položaj vrata pokazuje kako su kotorska utvrđenja povezivala bedeme, vodotokove i kontrolisane prolaze u jedinstven odbrambeni sistem.

## 21. The Škurda River / Rijeka Škurda

**Zona:** Sjeverna vrata i Škurda · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 59 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The Škurda River marks a part of Kotor's northern edge and forms a distinctive element of its defensive landscape. The town's walls were positioned to take advantage of the mountain, the bay and the local watercourses. This combination helps explain why the fortification system was extended and modified from approximately the ninth to the eighteenth century. Water also influenced the ordinary life of the settlement. The town depended on access to safe supplies, while canals, outlets and bridges affected movement and maintenance. The significance of Kotor's setting is recognized in its UNESCO World Heritage designation of 1979. UNESCO describes not only monuments but also the relationship between the coastal settlements and a bay enclosed by steep mountains. The Škurda is one small but visible part of that larger relationship between geography and architecture.

**ME — naracija**

Rijeka Škurda označava dio sjeverne granice Kotora i poseban je element njegovog odbrambenog pejzaža. Gradski zidovi postavljeni su tako da koriste položaj planine, zaliva i lokalnih vodotokova. Takav raspored pomaže da se razumije zašto je sistem utvrđenja proširivan i mijenjan približno od IX do XVIII vijeka. Voda je uticala i na svakodnevni život naselja. Grad je zavisio od pristupa izvorima i bunarima, dok su kanali, odvodnja i mostovi određivali kretanje i održavanje prostora. Vrijednost kotorskog okruženja prepoznata je upisom područja na UNESCO-vu Listu svjetske baštine 1979. godine. UNESCO ističe ne samo spomenike već i odnos naselja, zaliva i strmih planina. Škurda je jedan od vidljivih djelova te povezanosti geografije i arhitekture.

## 22. The Walls That Climb the Mountain / Bedemi koji se penju uz planinu

**Zona:** Kotorske zidine · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 48 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

The defensive walls rise above the Old Town towards Saint John's Fortress, about 260 metres above sea level. They are the result of many construction phases, roughly between the ninth and eighteenth centuries. The entire fortification line measures about 4.5 kilometres, according to the Municipality of Kotor. Some walls rise as much as twenty metres, while their thickness varies considerably according to location and function. These figures show the scale of the undertaking for a relatively compact port town. Fortifications had to protect the inhabited streets below and the mountain approaches above. During the period of Venetian government, from 1420 to 1797, Kotor formed part of a wider Adriatic defensive network. Today the walls are an essential feature of the World Heritage landscape, but their conservation remains a continuing responsibility rather than a completed task.

**ME — naracija**

Bedemi se iznad Starog grada penju prema tvrđavi Sveti Ivan, na oko 260 metara nadmorske visine. Nastajali su u mnogim građevinskim fazama, približno između IX i XVIII vijeka. Prema Opštini Kotor, ukupna dužina utvrđenja iznosi oko 4,5 kilometara. Pojedini zidovi dostižu visinu do dvadeset metara, dok im se širina znatno razlikuje prema položaju i namjeni. Ti podaci pokazuju razmjere gradnje za relativno malo lučko naselje. Utvrđenja su morala štititi naseljene ulice u podnožju i planinske prilaze iznad grada. Tokom mletačke uprave od 1420. do 1797. Kotor je bio dio šireg jadranskog sistema odbrane. Danas su bedemi neodvojiv dio pejzaža svjetske baštine, ali njihovo očuvanje ostaje stalan zadatak.

## 23. Karampana: Where Neighbours Met / Karampana: mjesto susreta komšija

**Zona:** Kotorske zidine · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 64 s / 54 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Karampana is the name of a historic public fountain and gathering place in Kotor's Old Town. Local accounts date the fountain to the seventeenth century. Before water was available inside every home, public water points played an important role in household routines and social contact. The location is remembered as a place where residents exchanged news as well as collected water. The name Karampana also has a place in Kotor's local humour and carnival culture: a satirical carnival paper of that name has reported on residents and events. These traditions should not be confused with a precise founding date for the fountain itself, which needs specialist verification. They do, however, reveal the function of a small public place in the life of a town whose streets were crowded with homes, workshops and shops. Karampana is part of Kotor's history of everyday community life.

**ME — naracija**

Karampana je naziv istorijske javne česme i mjesta okupljanja u Starom gradu. U lokalnim prikazima njeno postojanje vezuje se za XVII vijek. Prije nego što je voda bila dostupna u svakoj kući, javne česme bile su važne za kućne poslove i svakodnevne susrete. Karampana je u sjećanjima ostala i kao mjesto na kojem su se uz vodu razmjenjivale novosti. Isto ime ima i posebno mjesto u kotorskoj satiričnoj i karnevalskoj kulturi: list Karampana duhovito je komentarisao ljude i događaje u gradu. Te tradicije ne treba miješati sa preciznim, dokumentovanim datumom nastanka same česme, koji zahtijeva dodatnu stručnu provjeru. Ipak, pokazuju koliko je jedan mali javni prostor bio važan u gradu ispunjenom kućama, radionicama i butigama. Karampana čuva dio istorije kotorske svakodnevice.

## 24. Palaces and Family Names / Palate i porodična imena

**Zona:** Južni dio Starog grada · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 50 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's noble palaces record the names of families who held civic and commercial influence. The Drago Palace, facing Saint Tryphon's Square, has origins in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries and preserves Gothic features. The family's coat of arms includes a dragon, a detail reflected in the stone decoration. The Bizanti Palace dates from the fourteenth century and was extensively altered after the earthquake of 1667; the date 1674 is connected with its rebuilding. The Pima Palace belongs to the seventeenth century and is noted for its richly worked balcony and family coat of arms. These buildings are not interchangeable examples of wealth. Each has a different construction history, decorative vocabulary and relationship with the surrounding streets. Together they demonstrate how noble households commissioned local craftsmen and helped shape the appearance of Kotor over several centuries.

**ME — naracija**

Kotorske plemićke palate čuvaju imena porodica koje su imale značajan privredni i javni uticaj. Palata Drago, na Trgu Svetog Tripuna, potiče iz XIV i XV vijeka i sačuvala je gotičke elemente. Porodični grb nosi motiv zmaja koji se odražava i u kamenoj dekoraciji. Palata Bizanti nastala je u XIV vijeku, a temeljno je preuređivana poslije zemljotresa 1667; sa obnovom je povezana 1674. godina. Palata Pima pripada XVII vijeku i poznata je po bogato obrađenom balkonu i porodičnom grbu. Ove zgrade nijesu međusobno zamjenljivi primjeri bogatstva. Svaka ima drugačiju istoriju gradnje, ukrasa i odnos prema okolnim ulicama. Zajedno pokazuju kako su plemićke kuće angažovale lokalne majstore i oblikovale izgled grada tokom više vjekova.

## 25. Flour Square and the Pima Palace / Pjaca od brašna i palata Pima

**Zona:** Južni dio Starog grada · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 59 s / 51 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Flour Square, known locally as Pjaca od brašna, takes its name from the everyday trade in food and provisions. Its outstanding architectural landmark is the Pima Palace, a seventeenth-century residence with Renaissance and Baroque features. A broad stone balcony rests on twelve carved brackets; its iron railing is an example of local metalworking. Above the main entrance, two sculpted angels support the Pima family coat of arms. The palace was restored after the earthquake of 1979. Nearby stood other houses connected with families active in Kotor's trade and public life. The square thus brings together two sides of urban history: the circulation of basic goods and the display of wealth by prominent households. Both depended on the work of the town's craftsmen, carriers, traders and suppliers, whose names rarely appear in tourist histories.

**ME — naracija**

Pjaca od brašna dobila je ime po svakodnevnoj trgovini hranom i potrepštinama. Njena najupadljivija građevina je Palata Pima, rezidencija iz XVII vijeka sa renesansnim i baroknim elementima. Široki kameni balkon oslanja se na dvanaest obrađenih konzola, a gvozdena ograda primjer je rada kotorskih kovača. Iznad glavnog ulaza dva isklesana anđela pridržavaju grb porodice Pima. Palata je obnovljena nakon zemljotresa 1979. godine. U okolini su se nalazile i druge kuće porodica koje su učestvovale u trgovini i javnom životu Kotora. Trg tako spaja dva aspekta urbane prošlosti: promet osnovnih namirnica i pokazivanje bogatstva uglednih domaćinstava. I jedno i drugo zavisilo je od rada zanatlija, nosača, trgovaca i dobavljača, čija imena rijetko ulaze u turističke vodiče.

## 26. Craftspeople Who Shaped the City / Zanatlije koje su oblikovale grad

**Zona:** Južni dio Starog grada · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 64 s / 52 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's buildings reveal the work of stonecutters, carpenters, goldsmiths and other skilled craftspeople. UNESCO specifically highlights the medieval masonry and painting traditions of the region. A related craft developed in Dobrota, the maritime settlement beside Kotor: Dobrota lace, a distinctive form of hand embroidery. Historical examples were used for ceremonial cloths, household textiles and church furnishings. The technique is closely associated with the women of seafaring families and was passed from generation to generation. Its development reflects local adaptations of embroidery and lace-making traditions circulating around the Adriatic. The skill of making Dobrota lace is officially protected as an element of Montenegro's intangible cultural heritage. Surviving pieces are preserved in church and museum collections, while contemporary workshops continue to teach the technique. The craft forms an important part of the region's history alongside the stone palaces and ships for which Kotor is better known.

**ME — naracija**

Kotorske građevine svjedoče o radu klesara, tesara, zlatara i drugih vještih zanatlija. UNESCO posebno ističe srednjovjekovne tradicije klesarstva i slikarstva na ovom području. U susjednoj Dobroti, važnom pomorskom naselju, razvila se dobrotska čipka, prepoznatljiv oblik ručnog veza. Sačuvani primjerci korišćeni su za svečane marame, kućni tekstil i crkvene predmete. Umijeće se vezuje za žene pomorskih porodica i prenosilo se sa generacije na generaciju. U njegovom razvoju prepoznaju se lokalne prilagodbe čipkarskih tehnika koje su kružile Jadranom. Vještina izrade dobrotske čipke zvanično je zaštićena kao nematerijalno kulturno dobro Crne Gore. Vrijedni primjerci čuvaju se u crkvenim i muzejskim zbirkama, dok savremene radionice nastavljaju da prenose znanje. Uz palate i pomorsku istoriju, čipka čini važan dio kulturne baštine Boke.

## 27. Everyday Kotor: Cats and Traditional Sweets / Svakodnevni Kotor: mačke i tradicionalni kolači

**Zona:** Južni dio Starog grada · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 71 s / 56 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Cats are an unofficial emblem of Kotor, but the city's everyday heritage also includes its traditional desserts. Kotorska pašta, the local version of a custard slice or krempita, is remembered for its layers of crisp pastry and yellow vanilla cream. It became especially associated with the former Zdravljak pastry shop near Saint Tryphon's Square, where generations of residents bought the sweet. The traditional recipe uses three pastry layers. Efforts to preserve and teach the old recipe included workshops held in 2019. Another speciality comes from nearby Dobrota: Dobrota cake, made with almonds, eggs and ingredients such as maraschino liqueur and lemon zest. Traditionally it was prepared for weddings and major family or religious celebrations, including Christmas and Easter. The skill of making Dobrota, also known as Dobrota–Perast, cake is officially listed as Montenegro's intangible cultural heritage. These desserts record the tastes, ingredients and family customs of the Bay of Kotor as clearly as its palaces record the town's architecture.

**ME — naracija**

Mačke su postale nezvanični simbol Kotora, ali svakodnevno nasljeđe grada čuvaju i tradicionalni kolači. Kotorska pašta, domaća krempita, prepoznatljiva je po slojevima prhkog tijesta i žutoj kremi od vanile. Posebno se pamti nekadašnji Zdravljak u blizini Trga Svetog Tripuna, gdje su generacije Kotorana kupovale ovu poslasticu. Tradicionalna receptura podrazumijeva tri kore. Radi očuvanja starog recepta organizovane su i radionice, među njima 2019. godine. Iz obližnje Dobrote potiče dobrotska torta, pripremana od badema, jaja i sastojaka poput maraskina i limunove korice. Nekada se pravila za vjenčanja, Božić, Uskrs i druge važne porodične proslave. Vještina izrade dobrotske, odnosno dobrotske/peraške torte, zvanično je zaštićena kao nematerijalno kulturno dobro Crne Gore. Ovi kolači svjedoče o ukusima, namirnicama i porodičnim običajima Boke jednako kao što njene palate govore o istoriji graditeljstva.

## 28. The Earthquake of 1979 and Recovery / Zemljotres 1979. i obnova

**Zona:** Završetak ture · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 57 s / 49 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

On 15 April 1979, a powerful earthquake struck the Montenegrin coast and seriously damaged Kotor's historic buildings. Churches, palaces, town walls and museum collections all required attention. The earthquake coincided with an important international decision: in 1979, UNESCO inscribed the Natural and Culturo-Historical Region of Kotor on the World Heritage List. The property was also placed on the List of World Heritage in Danger, where it remained until 2003. Conservation involved international and local cooperation, including the establishment of a municipal heritage-protection institute in 1980. The Maritime Museum's Grgurina Palace underwent major restoration between 1982 and 1984, allowing its collections to return to public display. The recovery was therefore more than a physical rebuilding campaign. It also changed how the city documented, managed and protected its historic environment.

**ME — naracija**

Snažan zemljotres pogodio je crnogorsko primorje 15. aprila 1979. godine i teško oštetio istorijske građevine Kotora. Crkve, palate, gradski bedemi i muzejske zbirke zahtijevali su sanaciju. Iste godine donesena je važna međunarodna odluka: UNESCO je Prirodno i kulturno-istorijsko područje Kotora upisao na Listu svjetske baštine. Područje je istovremeno stavljeno na Listu ugrožene svjetske baštine, na kojoj je ostalo do 2003. godine. Konzervacija je zahtijevala međunarodnu i lokalnu saradnju, uključujući osnivanje opštinskog zavoda za zaštitu nasljeđa 1980. godine. Palata Grgurina, u kojoj se nalazi Pomorski muzej, temeljno je obnavljana od 1982. do 1984. godine. Oporavak zato nije bio samo građevinska obnova, već je promijenio i način dokumentovanja, upravljanja i zaštite kotorske baštine.

## 29. Kotor Today: The Story Continues / Kotor danas: priča se nastavlja

**Zona:** Završetak ture · **Procjena govora EN / ME:** 60 s / 54 s (135 riječi/min)

**EN — narration**

Kotor's historic centre is the result of more than a thousand years of building, trade, worship and cultural change. Its early churches include the ninth-century sanctuary associated with Saint Tryphon, the cathedral consecrated in 1166 and the Church of Saint Luke from 1195. Venetian administration from 1420 to 1797 left a lasting mark on public buildings and palaces. The earthquake of 1979 led to large-scale restoration and the UNESCO inscription of the wider region. But the town's heritage is also maintained through living practices. The Boka Navy tradition was inscribed by UNESCO in 2021, while carnival customs are documented in Kotor's archives as early as 1508. Today, these monuments and traditions coexist with ordinary residential life and tourism. Preserving Kotor means protecting the buildings, supporting local communities and keeping the historical record accurate for future generations.

**ME — naracija**

Istorijsko jezgro Kotora nastajalo je tokom više od hiljadu godina gradnje, trgovine, vjerskog života i kulturnih promjena. Među ranim crkvama su svetilište Svetog Tripuna iz IX vijeka, katedrala osvećena 1166. i Crkva Svetog Luke iz 1195. godine. Mletačka uprava od 1420. do 1797. ostavila je trajan trag na javnim zgradama i palatama. Zemljotres iz 1979. doveo je do velike obnove i upisa šireg područja Kotora na UNESCO-vu Listu svjetske baštine. Gradsko nasljeđe, međutim, čuvaju i živi običaji. UNESCO je 2021. upisao tradiciju Bokeljske mornarice, dok se kotorski karneval pominje u arhivskim dokumentima još 1508. godine. Danas spomenici i običaji postoje uporedo sa svakodnevnim životom stanovnika i turizmom. Očuvanje Kotora znači zaštitu građevina, podršku lokalnoj zajednici i tačno prenošenje istorijskih činjenica budućim generacijama.
