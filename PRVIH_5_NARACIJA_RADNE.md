# Kotor Stories — prvih pet originalnih naracija (radne verzije)

## 01 — The Sea Gate / Morska vrata

**English, draft (~60 s)**

Welcome to Kotor. Before you enter, look up at the Sea Gate and the strong stone walls around it. The gateway you see today dates from 1555, during the city's long Venetian chapter. For centuries, the sea was not merely Kotor's view. It was the road that connected this town with other ports, traders, sailors and new ideas. Imagine stepping through these gates when arriving meant coming from a ship, rather than from a car or a cruise terminal. The stone is still here, but every generation has given this entrance a new meaning. When you're ready, walk through into the Square of Arms. Your journey through Old Kotor begins here.

**Crnogorski, radna verzija (~60 s)**

Dobro došli u Kotor. Prije nego što uđete u grad, podignite pogled prema Morskim vratima i masivnim kamenim bedemima. Današnji izgled vrata potiče iz 1555. godine, iz vremena mletačke vlasti. More za Kotor nikada nije bilo samo lijep pogled. Bilo je put koji je grad povezivao sa lukama, trgovcima, pomorcima i novim idejama. Zamislite kako je izgledao dolazak kada su putnici ovdje pristizali brodom, a ne automobilom ili kruzerom. Kamen je ostao isti, ali su se vremena mijenjala. Prođite kroz vrata i polako uđite na Trg od oružja. Ovdje počinje naša šetnja.

## 02 — Clock Tower / Sat kula

**English, draft (~55 s)**

You have arrived at the Square of Arms, one of the most recognizable meeting places in Kotor. Take a moment to observe the Clock Tower. In a town once shaped by ships, crafts and markets, public time mattered. A clock on a central square did more than tell the hour: it helped organize the rhythm of daily life. Look around at the buildings framing the square. They did not all appear at once, and their details remind us that Kotor's history is made of many different periods. Today the square is full of conversations, footsteps and visitors. It is easy to imagine that, centuries ago, it was just as lively in a very different way.

**Crnogorski, radna verzija (~55 s)**

Stigli ste na Trg od oružja, jedno od najpoznatijih mjesta okupljanja u Kotoru. Zastanite i pogledajte Sat kulu. U gradu koji je vjekovima živio od pomorstva, zanata i trgovine, javno mjerenje vremena imalo je važnu ulogu. Sat na glavnom trgu nije samo pokazivao koliko je sati: pomagao je da se uskladi svakodnevni život. Pogledajte fasade koje okružuju trg. Nijesu nastale odjednom, već nose tragove različitih epoha. Danas se ovdje miješaju jezici turista i razgovori mještana. Pokušajte da zamislite kakva je vreva na istom mjestu vladala prije nekoliko vjekova.

## 03 — Saint Tryphon Cathedral / Katedrala Svetog Tripuna

**English, draft (~70 s)**

Stand for a moment in front of the Cathedral of Saint Tryphon. The building was consecrated in 1166, making it one of Kotor's great witnesses to the Middle Ages. The cathedral is dedicated to the city's patron saint. But what you see is not simply a building frozen in the twelfth century. Earthquakes and later rebuilding changed parts of its appearance over time. That is why this façade carries the marks of more than one period of history. Notice the two towers, the stonework and the open space of the square. These are not just details for a photograph: they show how the city invested care, skill and faith in a place that still matters to its community today.

**Crnogorski, radna verzija (~70 s)**

Zastanite ispred Katedrale Svetog Tripuna. Ova građevina osveštana je 1166. godine i jedan je od najznačajnijih svjedoka srednjovjekovne istorije Kotora. Posvećena je zaštitniku grada, Svetom Tripunu. Međutim, pred vama nije spomenik koji je ostao potpuno nepromijenjen od dvanaestog vijeka. Zemljotresi, obnova i kasnije intervencije mijenjali su pojedine dijelove njenog izgleda. Zato na fasadi možemo prepoznati tragove više istorijskih epoha. Obratite pažnju na dva zvonika, kamene detalje i trg ispred katedrale. To nijesu samo lijepi motivi za fotografiju: oni govore o vještini, vjeri i posebnom mjestu koje ova građevina ima u životu grada.

## 04 — The Maritime Museum / Pomorski muzej

**English, draft (~65 s)**

You are now near Kotor's Maritime Museum, housed in the Baroque palace of the Grgurina family. The museum grew out of a collection created by the Boka Marine fraternity around 1880 and opened to the public at the beginning of the twentieth century. That story tells us something important: the people of the bay cared deeply about preserving the memory of their ships and sailors. Kotor was connected to the wider Adriatic through maritime trade, navigation and families whose lives crossed the sea. Even if you do not go inside today, pause here and imagine the maps, instruments, portraits and journeys that help bring this history to life. The sea is part of the city's identity, even among these stone streets.

**Crnogorski, radna verzija (~65 s)**

Nalazite se u blizini Pomorskog muzeja, smještenog u baroknoj palati porodice Grgurina. Muzej je nastao iz zbirke koju je Bokeljska mornarica počela da stvara oko 1880. godine, a javnosti je predstavljena početkom dvadesetog vijeka. Već ta činjenica govori koliko su Bokelji željeli da sačuvaju uspomene na svoje brodove i pomorce. Kotor je preko mora bio povezan sa širim Jadranom, trgovinom i porodicama čiji su životi prolazili kroz mnoge luke. Čak i ako danas ne ulazite u muzej, zastanite i zamislite pomorske karte, instrumente, portrete i putovanja koja čine ovu istoriju. More je dio identiteta Kotora čak i duboko među njegovim kamenim ulicama.

## 05 — Saint Luke's Church / Crkva Svetog Luke

**English, draft (~60 s)**

In this intimate square stands the Church of Saint Luke. An inscription places its construction in 1195. The building brings together architectural influences associated with both the Romanesque and Byzantine traditions. Kotor's history is not the story of a single ruler or a single community. It is a city where different cultural currents met in streets, workshops, homes and places of worship. Notice how modest the scale of this church feels beside the monumental cathedral you saw earlier. Both belong to the same city, and both carry centuries of history. Before moving on, look around this little square and notice how closely sacred buildings and everyday life share the space.

**Crnogorski, radna verzija (~60 s)**

Na ovom malom trgu nalazi se Crkva Svetog Luke. Prema natpisu na građevini, podignuta je 1195. godine. U njenoj arhitekturi prepoznaju se uticaji romaničke i vizantijske tradicije. Istorija Kotora nije priča o samo jednom vladaru ili jednoj zajednici. Ovdje su se vjekovima susretali različiti kulturni uticaji, u ulicama, radionicama, kućama i bogomoljama. Obratite pažnju koliko ova crkva djeluje skromno u poređenju sa velikom katedralom koju ste ranije vidjeli. Ipak, obje pripadaju istom gradu i čuvaju mnogo vjekova istorije. Prije nego što krenete dalje, pogledajte kako se na ovom trgu svakodnevni život i istorija gotovo dodiruju.
